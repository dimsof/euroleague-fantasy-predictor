Player status color guide

Ready The player is fully ready to play.
Expected The player is expected to play, but it's not confirmed.
Questionable There is a 50-50 chance about the player's availability.
Game-time The final decision will be made close to game time.
Doubtful The player is unlikely to play, but there is still a chance.
Out The player will not play.
Uncertain The player's status is unclear or still unknown.
